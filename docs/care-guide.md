# Care and safety guide

Natural Sciences and Technology includes fire and electricity. The app teaches those ideas but never sends a child alone toward a flame, hot surface or wall socket.

- Fire, burning, heat and mains electricity are knowledge topics only unless a trusted grown-up is present.
- A hands-on call uses only the allowed kit in `do-it-yourself.md`.
- The app never claims that a phone performed an experiment or that a result happened on screen.
- Safety is spoken before the practical steps.
- A child records what they actually observe; the app never supplies a pretend result.
