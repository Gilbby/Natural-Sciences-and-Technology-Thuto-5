# Hands-on materials

Allowed materials for solo activities:

- paper
- pencil
- straws
- tape
- soil
- water
- magnet
- nail
- torch cell
- wire
- bulb
- elastic band
- card
- play-dough

Fire, matches, candles, stoves, hot surfaces, plugs and wall sockets are never solo materials. A practical involving heat or mains electricity is watched with a trusted grown-up and the child does not handle the source of heat or mains power.
