# Natural Sciences and Technology facts register

Claims in lessons point to one of these references. Sources are the DBE 2026 Annual Teaching Plan and standard school science references.

- `living-habitats` - A habitat is a place where a plant or animal lives. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `vertebrate-groups` - Fish, birds, reptiles, amphibians and mammals are vertebrate groups. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `south-african-indigenous-life` - South Africa has a wide variety of indigenous plants and animals. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `skeleton-protection` - The skull protects the brain; ribs help protect the heart and lungs. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `structures-frames-shells` - A skeleton is a frame structure and a crab shell is a shell structure. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `food-chain-roles` - Green plants make their own food using air, water and sunlight; animals depend on plants. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `life-cycle-stages` - Plants and animals grow and develop through stages. Source: DBE ATP, Life and Living.
- `metal-properties` - Metals have properties including strength, shine, conductivity, magnetism and malleability; iron can rust. Source: DBE ATP, Matter and Materials.
- `processed-materials` - People process materials by mixing, setting, drying or cooking to make new materials. Source: DBE ATP, Matter and Materials.
- `fuel-energy` - Fuels store energy and burning can release heat and light. Source: DBE ATP, Energy and Change.
- `simple-circuits` - A complete circuit gives electrical energy a closed path; mains sockets are not for children. Source: DBE ATP, Energy and Change.
- `stored-energy-movement` - Stretched elastic and compressed springs store energy that can cause movement. Source: DBE ATP, Energy and Change.
- `earth-year-day` - Earth takes about 365 days to orbit the Sun and about 24 hours to spin once. Source: DBE ATP, Planet Earth and Beyond.
- `soil-types` - Sandy, clayey and loamy soil have different properties and support life. Source: DBE ATP, Planet Earth and Beyond.
- `sedimentary-rocks` - Sedimentary rocks form in layers over a very long time; sandstone, shale and limestone are examples. Source: DBE ATP, Planet Earth and Beyond.
- `south-african-fossils` - South Africa has a rich fossil record, including fossils from the Cradle of Humankind. Source: DBE ATP, Planet Earth and Beyond.
